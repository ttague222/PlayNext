Seasonal picks and better sharing.

• New: seasonal picks on the Play tab, starting November 1. Each season brings a couple of collections made for it, like couch co-op for when everyone's over, and the app's colors change to match. Tap one for three games that fit.
• Sharing got a big upgrade: send all three of your picks as one card, with a link that shows friends exactly what you got.
• PlayNxt share links now open directly in the app.
• Horror for every mood. The Horror filter now shows up whatever mood you pick, and there are a lot more horror games to choose from.
• Small fixes and polish throughout.
