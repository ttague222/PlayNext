## Competitive Snapshot

### 1. Backloggd (backloggd.com)
- **What it does:** Game tracking — log what you've played, rate it, build a list
- **Positioning:** Letterboxd for games. Social + personal archive
- **PlayNxt edge:** Backloggd is for *after* you play. PlayNxt is for the moment *before*. No social friction, no lists to maintain — just an answer right now.

### 2. GG App (ggapp.io)
- **What it does:** Game library tracking + backlog management, native iOS/Android
- **Positioning:** Power user game manager; premium tier ($4.99/mo) for full features
- **PlayNxt edge:** GG is about organizing your history. PlayNxt ignores your history entirely — it only cares about your next 30–90 minutes. No paywall on core function.

### 3. YesChat "What Should I Play?" (yeschat.ai)
- **What it does:** AI chatbot game recommender — give it preferences, get suggestions backed by Metacritic/OpenCritic scores
- **Positioning:** AI-first discovery tool, driven by critic scores
- **PlayNxt edge:** YesChat returns browsing results. PlayNxt returns a *decision*. No walls of text, no external reviews to cross-check — just a confident pick with a clear why-now explanation.
