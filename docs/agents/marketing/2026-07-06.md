## Competitive Snapshot

### 1. Backlog Shuffle (backlogshuffle.com) — *direct competitor, watch closely*
- **What it does:** AI web app — connect Steam, pick mood + time window, get a recommendation; learns a genre-affinity profile over time (Pro tier).
- **Positioning:** "Smart Shuffle" for your Steam backlog; HowLongToBeat-backed session fit.
- **PlayNxt edge:** Backlog Shuffle **requires a Steam login and only sees your Steam library** — it's web-only and blind to console players. PlayNxt is native iOS/Android, needs no account, and covers Game Pass / PS Plus / console. We recommend *the right game*, not just *a game you already own*.

### 2. RollPlay / Pick-a-Game (getrollplay.com, pickaga.me)
- **What they do:** "Steam roulette" — randomly surface an unplayed title from your library.
- **Positioning:** Novelty randomizers to break paralysis.
- **PlayNxt edge:** Random ≠ right. These ignore time and mood entirely and give zero *why*. PlayNxt returns an explainable pick ("fits your 30 min, quit anytime"), which is what actually builds decision confidence.

### 3. Backloggd / Stash (game trackers) — *adjacent, growing fast*
- **What they do:** Log, rate, and organize games played (Backloggd hit ~650K users, dev went full-time).
- **Positioning:** "Letterboxd for games" — social archive of your history.
- **PlayNxt edge:** Trackers are for *after* you play. PlayNxt owns the moment *before*. Their growth proves the audience cares deeply about their libraries — a warm audience to reach with a "stop cataloging, start playing" message.
